DUO TROUBLE

Two Players. One Goal. Unlimited Trouble.

Duo Trouble is a fast-paced 2D cooperative platforming adventure where two players must work together, overcome dangerous obstacles and survive unpredictable challenges.

GAME BUILD NOT YET DEPLOYED

About this game

Duo Trouble drops two players into a stylized pixel-art world built entirely around cooperation. Every obstacle, trap and puzzle is tuned so that one player alone can't make it through — timing, communication and trust are the whole game.

The project is being built in Godot Engine with online co-op as a first-class feature, so you and a friend can play from different locations without split-screen or a shared device.
